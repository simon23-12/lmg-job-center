How do I research a job?
  1. Look up your job on willrobotstakemyjob.com – type the job title into the search box. Read what the job involves and how high the automation risk is.
  2. Don't just copy the percentage: find out why the risk is high or low. Which tasks could a machine do, and which can't it do?
  3. Look for news: Are there already robots or AI tools for this job?
  4. Write at least 2 arguments for and 2 arguments against replaceability.
  5. Decide: How long until AI/robots can do most of this job? Then write your advice for a person who wants this job.
